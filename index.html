Welcome!

Welcome to Kyiv Animal Shelter. We rescue dogs and cats, many from war‑affected areas, and provide them with safety, care, and love until they find a forever home. We are always grateful for any support — whether through volunteering, donations, or spreading the word. For safety reasons, we do not share our exact address publicly, but we welcome your help through trusted community channels.
